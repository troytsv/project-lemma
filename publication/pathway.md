# Publication pathway

Policy sources checked 25 November 2026. This plan is prepared for approval. The public links, tag, DOI, arXiv identifier, and approvals remain to be obtained.

## Recommended route

1. Review the complete proof, verify sources and licenses, and resolve whether the named human author meets the target's authorship criteria and is prepared to take responsibility. Record actual contributions and leave unverified declarations open.
2. Request a private expert assessment of the lower bound and literature priority after approval. The expert can receive the fixed PDF and an approved archive without waiting for a public URL. The separate author-review packet includes a draft request with space for the eventual fixed release links.
3. After approval, release `v1.0.0` of `project-lemma` on GitHub and deposit the exact reproducibility archive with Zenodo. Cite the version-specific DOI for reproducibility.
4. Seek arXiv endorsement for `math.CO` if required and post the source package after approval. Keep the manuscript and proof artifact separate, connected by versioned references.
5. Submit to *Designs, Codes and Cryptography* (DCC) via its subscription route, subject to author readiness and the editor's assessment of the disclosed AI role. DCC published the directly related Hall, Horsley and Stinson article. *The Electronic Journal of Combinatorics* (E-JC) is the fallback after the human author rewrites the AI-derived argument in their own words and personally checks every step. [DCC aims and scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/10623/aims-and-scope) · [2024 paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10623-024-01418-6) · [E-JC submission rules](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/about/submissions)

## Requirements and fees

### GitHub

A public repository and ordinary release are available on GitHub Free. The release tag identifies the source snapshot, which GitHub packages in ZIP and tar archives. Upload the curated proof archive and its checksum as explicit release assets if useful. GitHub permits up to 1,000 assets per release, each smaller than 2 GiB. Place `CITATION.cff` and the AI disclosure at the repository root. GitHub's ownership and content terms apply to uploaded material. No scholarly endorsement or author qualification is required for a regular release. [Plans](https://docs.github.com/en/get-started/learning-about-github/githubs-plans) · [Releases](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/releasing-projects-on-github/about-releases) · [Citation files](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/about-citation-files) · [Terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service)

### Zenodo

Zenodo is free for an ordinary deposit and defaults to a 50 GB quota per record. Use one software record for the reproducibility archive. Manual upload lets the depositor inspect the exact payload and metadata before publication. The GitHub integration automatically ingests new releases of enabled public repositories, and `.zenodo.json` takes precedence over `CITATION.cff`. Choose one route to avoid duplicate records. This repository's [draft metadata](zenodo.json) is in a subdirectory for manual use. Cite the DOI for the archived version and use the concept DOI when citing the evolving project. Add a related preprint ID or journal DOI once assigned. [Quota](https://help.zenodo.org/docs/deposit/manage-quota/) · [Deposit](https://help.zenodo.org/docs/get-started/quickstart/) · [Versioning](https://help.zenodo.org/docs/deposit/manage-versions/) · [GitHub integration](https://help.zenodo.org/docs/github/enable-repository/) · [Metadata precedence](https://help.zenodo.org/docs/github/describe-software/)

Zenodo requires accountable depositors and creators, prohibits listing AI tools as creators or contributors, and expects substantive AI use in the record description itself. Its guidance excludes AI output with no verifiable basis in human research. Have the depositor review the scope of human work and the description before submitting. If suitability is still uncertain, seek an eligibility answer before depositing. The Lean build is evidence about specified formal statements. Eligibility and authorship remain separate decisions. [Generative AI depositor policy](https://support.zenodo.org/help/en-gb/13-policies/227-what-is-your-usage-policy-for-generative-ai-for-depositors)

### arXiv

`math.CO` includes combinatorial designs, graphs, and hypergraphs, making it the best primary category. Standard author submission is free. Use a real identity and accurate affiliation information. The current rules require first-time submitters or users entering a new endorsement domain to qualify for automatic endorsement or obtain personal endorsement. Mathematics tightened the automatic route in December 2025. New submitters using that route need both an institutional email and prior authorship on an arXiv paper in the relevant endorsement domain. An unaffiliated first-time submitter should plan on requesting personal endorsement through arXiv's workflow. Check the potential endorser's eligibility on a relevant arXiv abstract page. Endorsement enables submission, which remains subject to moderation. [Category taxonomy](https://arxiv.org/category_taxonomy#math.CO) · [Identity and affiliation](https://info.arxiv.org/help/policies/identity_and_affiliation.html) · [Endorsement](https://info.arxiv.org/help/endorsement.html) · [January 2026 update](https://blog.arxiv.org/2026/01/21/attention-authors-updated-endorsement-policy/) · [Moderation](https://info.arxiv.org/help/moderation/index.html)

Upload self-contained LaTeX sources with figures, required style files, and the bibliography files used by the selected workflow. Check arXiv's built PDF and author metadata in the submission preview. Its published agreement requires authority to grant rights and imposes responsibility for the submission. arXiv requires authors to report significant use of text-to-text generative AI in the manuscript according to the field's methodology standards. AI tools cannot be listed as authors. Each listed author takes individual responsibility for all contents, including any AI-derived errors, attribution issues, or inaccurate references. Preserve the full disclosure and check the live submission prompts before finalizing. Published versions remain available as a permanent record. [TeX instructions](https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit_tex.html) · [Submittal agreement](https://info.arxiv.org/help/policies/submission_agreement.html) · [Generative AI policy](https://info.arxiv.org/help/moderation/index.html#policy-for-authors-use-of-generative-ai-language-tools) · [Policy announcement](https://blog.arxiv.org/2023/01/31/arxiv-announces-new-policy-on-chatgpt-and-similar-tools/)

### Designs, Codes and Cryptography

DCC explicitly accommodates unaffiliated authors and describes capturing their city and country of residence. Provide the corresponding author's active email in the submission system and review how the journal will display it. Submit editable LaTeX sources and a compiled PDF. The Springer Nature LaTeX template is recommended. The abstract should be 150–250 words with four to six keywords supplied separately. Include MSC codes, acknowledgments, a data or artifact availability statement as applicable, and the requested declarations. Enter author contributions and competing interests in the submission interface. [DCC submission guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/10623/submission-guidelines)

DCC excludes LLMs as authors and requires disclosure of use beyond copyediting in Methods or a suitable equivalent section. Springer recommends substantial scholarly contribution, drafting or critical revision, final approval, and accountability as authorship criteria where field-specific guidance is unavailable. Review the actual human contribution against those criteria before submission. The public guidelines do not give a categorical ruling on a proof generated primarily by AI under human direction. Ask the editor about fit if the disclosed contribution leaves uncertainty. [DCC authorship and AI rules](https://link.springer.com/journal/10623/submission-guidelines)

The subscription option carries no open-access APC. The optional open-access APC shown on 25 November 2026 was USD 3,290, GBP 2,390, or EUR 2,690, plus applicable taxes, priced at acceptance. Springer Nature allows preprints before journal submission. Check the actual publishing agreement before accepting terms. [DCC publishing options and fees](https://link.springer.com/journal/10623/how-to-publish-with-us) · [Springer Nature preprint policy](https://support.springernature.com/en/support/solutions/articles/6000258807-preprints)

### The Electronic Journal of Combinatorics

E-JC covers discrete mathematics, charges authors and readers USD 0, and lets authors retain copyright. The initial submission is PDF with a self-contained abstract. Final accepted sources use `e-jc.sty`. Its form asks for an affiliation and email, and the public instructions give no institutional eligibility restriction or details on the unaffiliated form. Enter truthful information and check with the journal if the form requires an institution. It requires personal verification and rewriting in the human author's own words for an AI-derived proof, issues an exclusive-submission declaration, and limits author submissions to one per three calendar months. The authors also agree that editors may use AI for a final check. Confirm the treatment of a posted preprint if choosing E-JC. [E-JC homepage](https://www.combinatorics.org/) · [Submissions](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/about/submissions) · [AI policy](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/about)

## Licensing and citation

The existing MIT grant covers the original project sources and accompanying writing. Gordon archive records and Canli’s data retain their CC BY 4.0 terms and attribution. The deposit draft identifies both terms. Before choosing journal or preprint terms, check compatibility with these existing grants and confirm that the author can grant the requested rights. The arXiv perpetual non-exclusive distribution license is the proposed choice subject to that check. ## Formal mathematics discoverability

GitHub and Zenodo are the primary references for the proof artifact. [Formal Conjectures](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures) accepts proposals for formal conjecture statements, supports an external Lean proof reference, and defines its solved category by expert acceptance of an informal solution. Check eligibility after review before proposing Füredi's conjecture or the solved special case. Lean's [Mathlib indexes](https://leanprover-community.github.io/documentation.html) have scope boundaries such as the 100 or 1000+ named theorem lists. [Reservoir](https://github.com/leanprover/reservoir) indexes Lean packages and build information. It is relevant only if the Lake package meets its discovery rules. The [Annals of Formalized Mathematics](https://afm.episciences.org/page/instructions-for-authors) is a journal option for a distinct substantial formalization article, and requires manuscript and code AI disclosure. Check its overlap rules before considering that route.
