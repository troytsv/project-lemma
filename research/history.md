# Research history

The first stage treated $C(70,21,2)$ directly. Its proof reduced a hypothetical fourteen-block covering to a rank profile whose final load had impossible parity. The complete original statement, proof and bundled prior certificate remain reproducible. The archived manuscript and original repository configuration can be read at commit `034715f2e4e1f96bde8652b5d49cfbea4ad7384b`. [Separate Lean proof](../formal/Lemma.lean) · [Original replay receipt](../formal/REPLAY.txt)

The second stage asked whether the projective-plane load $4/13$ on fourteen coordinates could be improved. A local search recovered a weighted system of mass 69 and capacity 21. Its ordinary covering proved isomorphic to Colin Barker's 2008 record. Exact inequalities on the weights subsequently gave the matching universal lower bound. This stage is preserved in commit `b951f630cf27a8158c79ee67c56bbad8491d5138`. [Search runs and seeds](../l14/classify/search-run.txt) · [Construction and prior work](../l14/prior-work.md)

The consolidated publication treats the sharp first Mills number as the primary theorem and the 70-point covering number as a corollary. The earlier conjectural route and dual bylines are historical. The current disclosure records the division of human, AI and machine-verification contributions. [Disclosure](../AI_DISCLOSURE.md)
