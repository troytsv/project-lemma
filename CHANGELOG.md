## mills-14-v1.0.0 — public preprint, 25 November 2026

Consolidates the first Mills number and pair-covering manuscripts. The primary theorem gives $L(14)=7/23$ and $\mu(14)=23/7$. The covering-number threshold, two replication families, and comparative data appear as its consequences. Includes machine-checked statements, a clean-build audit, attribution of the published upper bounds, an adversarial proof review, a qualified novelty assessment and a human-readable proof guide. The consolidated manuscript records substantive AI contributions in its declarations and lists the author, version, release date, data availability and author-provided declarations. The release adds the ten-row supplementary table and organizes research by problem.
