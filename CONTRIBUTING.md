# Contributing corrections and independent checks

A useful review identifies the theorem or manuscript passage, the precise mathematical claim at issue and a proof, counterexample, citation, or reproducible failure. For a Lean failure include the toolchain, platform, first diagnostic and the command that produced it. For an earlier result give a theorem or page number and explain how its hypotheses match this problem.

Use the pinned toolchain and run the certificate and formal checks in the [README](README.md). Keep the covering certificates traceable to their sources. Include the source, attribution and license for additional third-party data. Document any material AI assistance and identify the human contributor who has reviewed the proposed change. The maintainer will review corrections before changing mathematical statements, provenance or release status.

The project welcomes independent verification of the lower bound and references bearing on priority. Scholarly assessment, repository contributions and arXiv endorsement are separate matters.
